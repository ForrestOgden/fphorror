# NULL FLOOR — launch and marketing plan

## Product positioning

**One-sentence hook:** Every floor remembers what you did on the last one.

Sell the game as a compact, high-intensity psychological horror experience rather than a long indie campaign. The demo should end exactly when the player has learned the rules and the threat becomes aggressive.

## Price

- Browser/itch full game target: **$6.99**
- Intro launch price: **$4.99** for the first 72 hours
- Free demo: floors 08 through 01, ending on the Floor 00 reveal
- Full version target: 35–60 minutes, multiple anomaly sequences, 3 endings, replay modifiers

## Marketing loop

The core acquisition strategy is short-form video. Do not market with generic corridor walking. Every clip needs one immediately readable anomaly.

### Clip 1 — "The portrait"
1. Walk past portrait.
2. Turn around.
3. Eyes have followed the player.
4. Hard cut before the scare.
5. Text: **THE FLOOR WATCHES BACK.**

### Clip 2 — "Apartment 013"
1. Show door numbers in sequence.
2. Camera reaches impossible `013` door.
3. Whisper begins.
4. Door handle moves.
5. Cut to logo.

### Clip 3 — "Flashlight rule"
1. Entity stands at end of corridor.
2. Player shines flashlight: it stops.
3. Player turns flashlight off for one second.
4. Turn it back on: entity is much closer.
5. Text: **LIGHT KEEPS IT STILL.**

### Clip 4 — "Don't turn around"
1. Writing on wall: DON'T TURN AROUND.
2. Footsteps behind player.
3. Player slowly starts turning.
4. Cut before reveal.

Post variants to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X, and horror-game subcommunities. Test the first two seconds aggressively; retention matters more than cinematic intros.

## Funnel

Short clip → free browser demo → Floor 00 paywall → $6.99 purchase.

The browser demo removes installation friction. The strongest-performing clips can later drive to an itch.io page or Steam demo/wishlist page.

## Store copy

### Short description

The elevator only goes down. Cross a decaying apartment corridor, survive the things that change between floors, and learn the rules before the building learns you.

### Feature bullets

- Reactive first-person psychological horror
- Floors that mutate as you descend
- A stalking entity governed by light and line of sight
- Environmental storytelling with no combat
- Procedural audio, dynamic lighting, and cinematic post-processing
- Multiple anomaly types designed for replayability

## Launch checklist

- Replace procedural placeholders selectively with licensed high-quality props.
- Add a proper title/logo key art package.
- Add 10–15 additional floor anomaly modules.
- Add gamepad support.
- Add save/checkpoint and analytics only after the core loop tests well.
- Add purchase entitlement verification after Stripe checkout.
- Record 20 short vertical clips before launch day.
- Create itch.io page and a standalone landing page.
- Only begin Steam submission after the demo proves people finish it and ask for more.
